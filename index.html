🧭 What is BMC Canvas?

BMC Canvas is a lightweight HTML application built for 2026. It helps you create, arrange, and review Business Model Canvas information. You no longer need a whiteboard, sticky notes, or a complicated software install. This tool work directly in your browser or as a small local program on your Windows PC.

It is designed for a single purpose: to give you a clean, simple workspace where you can organize the nine key parts of your business model. The interface is easy to read and navigate. You can add text, move it around, and review your whole canvas at a glance.

📥 Getting Started

You can use BMC Canvas in two ways. First, you can open it right inside your browser without downloading anything. Second, you can download the Windows version to use it offline and store your files locally. Both versions work the same way. Here is how to start.
